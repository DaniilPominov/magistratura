The aim of this presentation is to introduce an IT service that helps small and medium-sized companies monitor and improve the performance of their databases. What I would like to talk about is why database reliability is critical for modern business and how a monitoring service can solve common technical problems.
As I said to begin with, a database is not only a technical component. It stores customers, orders, invoices, inventory, and internal reports. Therefore, if the database becomes slow or unavailable, business processes suffer immediately.
Firstly, the next point I would like to consider is the problem. Many companies use databases every day, but they do not monitor them systematically. Queries may become slow after data growth, locks may appear during peak load, and configuration errors may remain unnoticed. In many small organizations, specialists start investigating only after users complain. This approach is expensive, stressful, and often ineffective.
Then, the next point I would like to raise is the proposed service. The service continuously collects information about database health, query execution time, disk usage, failed connections, and blocking sessions. If an indicator exceeds a safe threshold, the system sends an alert to the administrator. In addition, it generates a weekly report with clear explanations and practical recommendations.
After that, moving on to the issue of technical implementation, the solution can be built using Python and .NET. Python is suitable for collectors, statistical analysis, and automation because it has strong libraries for database connectivity and data processing. The .NET layer can provide a stable service for scheduling, alerting, and report generation. The system can connect to SQL Server or PostgreSQL through standard drivers. A lightweight dashboard can show current status, historical trends, and unresolved incidents.
At the next stage, let us consider the business benefits. For technical staff, the service reduces the time needed to diagnose problems. For managers, it provides understandable reports about system stability and data availability. For the company as a whole, it lowers the risk of downtime, improves response time, and increases trust in analytics. As I mentioned earlier, manual checks are reactive, while automatic monitoring is preventive.
